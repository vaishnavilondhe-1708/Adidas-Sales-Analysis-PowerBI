# Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard 📊

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Adidas sales performance across products, retailers, regions, states, sales channels, and time periods.

![Adidas Sales Dashboard](screenshots/dashboard.png)

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard provides an interactive view of sales and profitability performance.

The dashboard allows users to explore sales trends and compare performance across different products, retailers, regions, states, months, years, and sales channels.

The goal of this project is to transform sales data into clear and actionable business insights using Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Track monthly sales trends
- Compare sales across different regions and states
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze retailer-wise sales performance
- Compare sales across different years and sales channels
- Create an interactive and easy-to-understand business dashboard

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data transformation and preparation
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs
- **Data Analysis** – Identifying trends and business insights

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard provides the following KPIs:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Units Sold
- Average Price per Unit
- Profit Margin

### Sales Analysis

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Region-wise Sales
- State-wise Sales
- Product-wise Sales
- Retailer-wise Sales

### Interactive Filters

Users can interact with the dashboard using:

- Month
- Year
- Sales Channel

These filters allow stakeholders to explore the data based on different business dimensions.

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- The **West region** contributes the largest share of overall sales.
- **Men's Street Footwear** is the highest-selling product category shown in the dashboard.
- **West Gear** has the highest retailer-wise sales.
- **April** records the highest monthly sales in the displayed period.
- The overall profit margin shown in the dashboard is **46.72%**.
